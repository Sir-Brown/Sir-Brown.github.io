Who am I?

I am D'Jaine Brown, a junior at Florida Atlantic University majoring in Computer Science with minors in Cybersecurity and Artificial Intelligence.

My interest in technology began with my mother and how she found ways to use it to make life easier. This birthed my interest in how technology can be used to solve problems, automate work, protect information, and create useful experiences.

Through my coursework, I've gained experience with several programming languages and areas of computer science, including software development, web development, data structures and algorithms, databases, and data analysis.

What I'm working toward

My primary career interests are software development and web development. At the same time, I'm building knowledge in cybersecurity, artificial intelligence, and automation because I believe these areas can complement the skills of a modern developer.

How I approach learning

One of my goals is to become a developer who can do more than simply write code that works. I want to understand why it works, recognize when it can be improved, and use modern tools effectively without becoming dependent on them.

I'm particularly interested in learning how to build efficient workflows, automate repetitive processes, and use AI as a development tool while still maintaining an understanding of the underlying technology.

What I bring to the table

I want to be someone a team can depend on.

Whether I'm contributing code, learning a new technology, solving a problem, or working with others, my goal is to provide meaningful value while continuing to improve.

This portfolio is part of that process. It represents not only what I can do today, but also the progress I'm making toward becoming the developer I want to be.
