About

Curious by nature. Practical by design.

I’m D’Jaine Brown, a Computer Science student at Florida Atlantic University, interested in building technology that solves real problems and makes everyday work more effective.

What draws me to technology

My interest in technology grew from seeing how it can make life easier. That curiosity developed into an interest in how software is designed, how systems work, and how thoughtful tools can reduce friction in everyday processes.

My studies have introduced me to programming, data structures and algorithms, databases, and software development. I’m especially interested in the intersection of software development, cybersecurity, artificial intelligence, and automation.

How I want to grow

I want to become a developer who understands more than the code directly in front of them: someone who can reason through a problem, learn unfamiliar tools, consider security and usability, and contribute reliably to a team.

This portfolio is a working record of that process. I’ll keep it grounded in what I have actually built and learned, while making room for the next things I want to explore.
